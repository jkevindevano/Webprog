About Me

Hi, I'm Jonathan Kevin Devano

I'm passionate about technology, digital creativity, and photography. I focus on building solutions that combine clean design, thoughtful structure, and meaningful impact both in digital products and visual storytelling.

With an analytical and strategic mindset, I enjoy transforming ideas into structured systems and capturing moments through a creative lens. For me, photography is not just about taking pictures, but about framing perspectives, emotions, and stories.

I believe the balance between logic and creativity is what turns simple concepts into powerful experiences. Through this website, I share my projects, visual works, and insights as I continue exploring technology, design, and photography.
